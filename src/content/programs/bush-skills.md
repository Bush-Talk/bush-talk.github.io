We offer a unique pathway to support personal development. Bush skills focuses on the
use of knowledge and skill with minimal reliance on equipment.

## How we spend our time

### Team Building Exercises

Through group exercises, fostering curiosity and self-reflective practices, participants
gain understanding of how they operate in a group. This understanding challenges them to
increase their social competence while being flexible in finding creative ways to solve
problems.

### Foraging and using natural resources

Learning about and working with natural resources increases our awareness, enjoyment and
appreciation of nature, changing the way we see the world around us.

### Circle time

Coming together and sharing as a group creates a great opportunity for deeper connection.
Participants are supported in developing their own skills in listening, articulating
thoughts and feelings and building respect for each other.

### Bush craft

Learning bush craft challenges participants to gain new skills and physical capabilities.
This provides opportunities for broadening problem solving skills, developing resilience,
self-reliance and responsible decision making.
