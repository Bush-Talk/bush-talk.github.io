Creating strong relationships and learning about how to cultivate healthier communication
has been a strong theme throughout my professional and personal life. My experience as a
mentor, counsellor and in education support, has strengthened my belief that helping
individuals form strong, healthy relationships directly improves overall happiness and
wellbeing.

Deepening my connection to nature and learning bush craft has been personally meaningful
and has led to an increase in confidence, peace and connection to the land I live on.
This inspired me to begin looking at ways I could combine teaching communication practices
with bush skills and natural learning.

In 2021, I began facilitating Bush Talk sessions in primary schools with a goal to
explicitly teach the skills of how to have more meaningful relationships. I have delighted
in watching participants growth in physical capability, confidence, respect and love in
the outdoors and time and time again, observing participants choose to increase
constructive and thoughtful behaviour and interactions brings me great joy.
